Services Listing

5-Star Concierge

Guests of The Americana at Brand have access to our award-winning Concierge Services. Our Concierge team is available to assist with any task. This complimentary resource offers guests the ultimate convenience, from gifting and shopping assistance to travel and meal reservations, as well as one-of-a-kind event planning.

Premium Gift Boxes

You've found the perfect present, now wrap it with love with our premium Gift Boxes, available at Caruso Concierge in two ready-to-gift sizes (medium: $8 and large: $10). Each comes with a card & envelope, tissue paper, and your choice of a red or silver ribbon.

Porter Service, Package Assistance and Hands-Free Shopping

Our porters are happy to help take the weight off through hands-free shopping. They can retrieve guests' shopping bags and either place them in their vehicle or securely store them for later pickup.

Restaurant and Travel Reservations

Caruso Concierge can secure dining reservations for The Americana at Brand or elsewhere in the city, as well as arrange transportation and plan guest vacations.

Assistance with Concert and Event Tickets

Our Caruso Concierge has access to many of the most popular concert and event venues as well as theme parks, attractions and tours across Southern California. We do our best to help guests reserve tickets to upcoming shows, can't-miss attractions and so much more. For theme parks, attractions and tours, please visit the website linked here.

Postage, Mail, Shipping and Delivery Assistance

Caruso Concierge is available to arrange various shipping and courier services, including Caruso Store-to-Door delivery from The Americana at Brand retailers. Please call 818.637.8982 or visit Caruso Concierge in The Grand Lobby for assistance.

Wheelchair, Umbrella, and Cell Phone Charger Rentals

Caruso Concierge provides complimentary wheelchair, umbrella and cell phone charger (ages 18+) rentals. To arrange, please visit the Caruso Concierge desk with a valid photo ID and debit/credit card. Guests will not be charged as long as items are returned at the end of their visit. Umbrellas are available for purchase at $25 each (tax not included).

Foreign Language and Tourism Assistance

We are happy to work with guests to enhance their overall stay in Los Angeles. Caruso Concierge will book hotels, make reservations and recommend nearby attractions. We can also provide guests with bilingual services at the desk, plus dozens of languages through KIPSU texting service.

Pet Policy

We are a pet-friendly destination and welcome all of your canine friends.

Assistance with Transportation Arrangements

Our Concierge can secure private and rideshare transportation services in the city.

ADA Services

  • ADA Accessible parking – Second floor
  • Wheelchair rental
  • Escort assistance on property by public safety
  • Designated ADA areas available at all events (first-come-first-serve)
  • Hearing devices are available upon request
  • ADA ramps located throughout the property

Gift Cards

Gift cards are available in amounts ranging from $20 – $1,000. They can be purchased at Caruso Concierge in The Grand Lobby.

Our Mastercard Gift Cards make a perfect gift for corporate incentive programs and special holidays.

Purchase fees apply per card. No ATM use. Funds do not expire. Not redeemable for cash, except where required by law.
